Develop a high-fidelity prototype for the SupplyProof app, ensuring a consistent and aesthetically pleasing design. Replace the temporary logo with the official one and use Tambay Spot images as a visual reference. 

Create a Login/Sign In page featuring a clean, modern design, with a consistent aesthetic. For the Sign Up page, include fields relevant to the app's purpose, such as business name, contact information, and industry type.

Project Overview:
- **SupplyProof**: A B2B delivery-verification tool for restaurant groups and retail businesses.
- **Core Problem**: Managing discrepancies in deliveries, such as missing or damaged items, and billing mismatches.
- **Solution**: A focused tool for documenting delivery issues, organizing evidence, and tracking resolutions.

Target Audience:
- Small to medium-sized businesses with regular supplier deliveries, specifically restaurant groups and regional retail chains.

Core Workflow:
1. **Verify Delivery**: Record ordered vs. received quantities.
2. **Document Discrepancies**: Flag issues with photos/notes.
3. **Check Evidence**: Ensure all information is ready before supplier contact.
4. **Track Resolution**: Log supplier responses and case status.
5. **Monitor Cases**: Provide management visibility into unresolved issues.

Proposed MVP Features:
- Delivery discrepancy entry form.
- Quantity comparison (expected vs. received).
- Evidence attachments and checklist.
- Discrepancy status and resolution tracking.
- Dashboard for open cases, disputed amounts, and recoveries.

Current Features:
- **Dashboard**: Displays open cases, disputed values, and recent discrepancies.
- **New Discrepancy**: Includes supplier, branch, PO reference, expected vs. received quantities, issue type, and notes/photos.
- **Case Details**: Features evidence checklist, current status, timeline, and resolution tracking.
- **Supplier Message**: Provides a copyable generated message with a return-to-case option.

This prototype will utilize manual entry and sample data, excluding complex integrations and automated communications.
